# Social, UAE, Europe and newsletter strategy

Briefs 25–28, 48–49.

## Social-first funnel

```
Reel / TikTok  →  bio link (one URL per campaign)  →  landing page in the viewer's language
   →  journey or destination  →  trip builder (pre-filled)  or  WhatsApp
```

- **Bio link:** use `insidephilippines.com/{landing}?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign={reel-slug}`. The site redirects to the right language automatically. Rotate the bio link to match the newest reel (Palawan reel → `/palawan`).
- **Stories:** link stickers straight to `/design-my-trip?destination=siargao&utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=story`.
- **The in-app browser** (Instagram/TikTok) is the main mobile environment: pages are static, light, no pop-ups, sticky CTA after the hero, WhatsApp opens the app directly.

@insidephilippines first (availability not verified). Fallbacks: @inside.philippines · @insidephilippines.travel · @insidephl · @theinsidephilippines. Same handle everywhere (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest).

**Languages:** produce once, subtitle four times (EN/FR/ES/RU burned-in captions; AR for UAE ads). Arabic voice-over for top-performing UAE ads.

**Cadence:** 4 reels + 3 carousels + daily stories per week; YouTube long-form monthly ("Inside Palawan: 7 days, honest"), cut into Shorts.

## UAE strategy (27)

- Pages: `/ar/luxury-philippines`, `/ar/family-philippines`, `/ar/honeymoon`, `/ar/private-villas`, `/ar/private-boats`, `/ar/luxury-island-escapes`, `/ar/wellness-journeys` (+ English versions).
- AED by default for UAE visitors; WhatsApp is a primary button on GCC landing heroes; Arabic pre-filled WhatsApp message.
- Messaging: privacy, private transfers, villas and boats, family logistics, concierge on the ground, honest notes on halal dining availability, direct Dubai/Abu Dhabi → Manila flights.
- Seasonality hook: UAE summer (Jun–Aug) is shoulder/wet season in much of the Philippines; recommend the east-coast exceptions and private, flexible itineraries honestly; push Dec–Apr for winter breaks and Eid dates.
- Channels: Instagram + Snapchat + Google Ads (Arabic + English, geo AE/SA/QA); WhatsApp Business catalogue with the 4 GCC journeys.
- Needs before launch: native Arabic copy review; a WhatsApp number answered in Arabic or a clear "we reply in English and Arabic within X hours".

## European strategy (28)

- Longer trips, earlier planning: journeys at 10, 12, 14, 15, 16 and 21 days; month pages publish 6–9 months ahead (French and Spanish book winter trips in summer).
- Education beats inspiration: travel-time content ("Why we don't do 5 islands in 14 days"), legs shown on every itinerary, "Ten Days, Two Islands" as the anti-overplanning route.
- FR/ES: lead with "circuit"/"ruta" language, EUR, long holidays (August, Christmas, Easter). Flag rainy season honestly for August and suggest the right coast.
- UK: "2 week Philippines itinerary", honeymoon, combine with Asia stopovers later.

## Newsletter: Inside Notes (49)

"A monthly letter from the islands." Signup in the footer of every page, plus after guide articles.
Structure: one route · one hotel discovery · one seasonal note · one place to eat · one story · CTA **Start planning your trip** → `/design-my-trip?utm_source=newsletter`.
Tooling: Brevo or Klaviyo (EU data residency, multilingual lists); connect via `NEWSLETTER_WEBHOOK_URL`. Segment by language from day one.
